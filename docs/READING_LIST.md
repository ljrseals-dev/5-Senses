# Recommended Reading — 1980s Classical AI & Context

## Foundational Neural Network Works

1. **Parallel Distributed Processing** (Volumes 1 & 2)  
   David E. Rumelhart, James L. McClelland & the PDP Research Group (1986)  
   Distributed representations, learning, and neural computation.

2. **Self-Organization and Associative Memory**  
   Teuvo Kohonen  
   Self-organizing maps and classical associative memory systems.

## Strategic & Institutional Context

3. **The Fifth Generation**  
   Edward Feigenbaum & Pamela McCorduck  
   Ambitions of 1980s knowledge-based and parallel systems.

4. **Strategic Computing** (DARPA program materials)  
   Government-funded machine intelligence research of the era.

5. **The Puzzle Palace** / **Body of Secrets**  
   James Bamford  
   Institutional structure, signals intelligence, and the culture of secrecy  
   (used here only as architectural *inspiration*, not as a technical blueprint).

## Additional Classical References

- Hopfield networks (associative memory)
- Boltzmann machines
- Early Hidden Markov Models for speech
- Expert systems literature (OPS5, MYCIN-style certainty factors)
- Statistical n-gram language models

These works form the intellectual foundation for the classical components of BODY OF SECRETS.
