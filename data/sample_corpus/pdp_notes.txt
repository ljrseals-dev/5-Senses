Parallel Distributed Processing (1986)

David Rumelhart, James McClelland and the PDP Research Group published two volumes that
re-introduced connectionism and distributed representations to the cognitive science and
AI communities.

Core ideas:
- Knowledge is stored in the connections (weights) among simple processing units.
- Learning occurs by adjusting those weights (e.g., the generalized delta rule / back-propagation).
- Representations are distributed rather than localist.
- The same network can exhibit rule-like behaviour without explicit symbolic rules.

These volumes are foundational reading for anyone studying classical neural networks
and the cognitive-science side of connectionism.
