Hopfield Networks (1982)

John Hopfield introduced a recurrent neural network model that functions as an associative memory.
Patterns are stored as local minima of an energy function. Retrieval proceeds by energy minimization.
The theoretical capacity of a Hopfield network with N neurons is approximately 0.15N random patterns
before spurious states become problematic.

The model uses a symmetric weight matrix with zero diagonal and bipolar or binary units.
Asynchronous update guarantees convergence to a stable state.

Key references:
- Hopfield, J. J. (1982). Neural networks and physical systems with emergent collective computational abilities.
- Amit, Gutfreund & Sompolinsky on statistical mechanics of Hopfield networks.
