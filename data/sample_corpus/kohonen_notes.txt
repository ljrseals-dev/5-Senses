Self-Organizing Maps (Kohonen)

Teuvo Kohonen developed the Self-Organizing Map (SOM), an unsupervised neural network that
performs topology-preserving dimensionality reduction.

Properties:
- Competitive learning (winner-take-all plus neighbourhood adaptation).
- The map preserves topological relationships present in the high-dimensional input.
- Widely used for visualisation, clustering and feature extraction in the 1980s and 1990s.

SOMs remain a classical alternative to supervised multilayer perceptrons when labelled
data is scarce or when exploratory analysis is the goal.
